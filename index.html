About Me

I am a fifth-year PhD student at the School of Mathematics, Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR), Mumbai. I am broadly interested in the interplay of Dynamics and Arithmetic. Before coming to TIFR, I completed my BS-MS from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Mohali.

Research

My research interests include:

  • Homogeneous dynamics and its applications to number theory
  • Extreme value problems arising in number theory and homogeneous dynamics
  • (Effective) equidistribution on homogeneous spaces, measure rigidity
